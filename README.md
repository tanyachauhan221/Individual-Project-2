
# Employee Compensation, Career Progression & Attrition Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
An individual data analytics project exploring employee compensation, career progression, workplace factors and attrition patterns using the IBM HR Analytics dataset.

## 2. Business Problem
Organisations need to understand how employee experience, job levels, compensation and workplace factors relate to employee outcomes to make informed workforce decisions.

## 3. Key Business Question
How do employee experience, career progression and organisational factors relate to monthly compensation, and what workforce patterns are associated with employee attrition?

## 4. Project Objectives
- Explore employee demographics and workforce characteristics.
- Examine factors associated with monthly income.
- Analyse compensation differences across job roles and levels.
- Investigate employee attrition patterns.
- Develop data-driven managerial recommendations.

## 5. Dataset
- Source: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset
- Records: 1,058
- Features: 35
- File: train.xlsx
- Target variable: MonthlyIncome
- Additional outcome: Attrition
